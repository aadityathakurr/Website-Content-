The framework we build on

What is the Moqui Framework?

Moqui is an open-source Java/Groovy framework for custom ERP, order management, and business automation. It provides an enterprise data model, reusable services, security, and multi-tenancy out of the box so you never start from scratch. Without infrastructure to build, engineers focus purely on unique business logic. Moqui adapts to any delivery model: building bespoke solutions, launching SaaS platforms, running ready-made apps, or powering headless backends.

Inside the framework

The four layers we build on

Underneath those applications sit four layers. This is where most custom work actually happens, and where twenty years of enterprise engineering earns its keep.

  1. 01

    Framework & Runtime

    A lightweight runtime handling security, multi-instance tenancy, caching and workflow in a 25 MB core. We tune it to the throughput you actually run, from single instances to clustered nodes.

  2. 02

    Mantle Universal Data Model

    390 entities covering orders, inventory, facilities, parties and financials. We map your processes onto them and extend only where you differ, inheriting two decades of enterprise data modeling.

  3. 03

    Universal Service Library

    Reusable services for order-to-cash, procure-to-pay and work-plan-to-pay, each with validation, transactions and authorization built in. We compose them into the logic that makes your operation different.

  4. 04

    API-First & Headless

    400+ REST endpoints, GraphQL and Apache Camel. Headless is the primary design pattern here, so we connect Moqui to the storefronts, ERPs and platforms you already run.

Moqui Framework, Mantle Business Artifacts and SimpleScreens are released to the public domain under Creative Commons Zero (CC0-1.0) with a Grant of Patent License. No fees, no seat counts, and no restriction on embedding it inside your own commercial product.

Frequently asked

Moqui questions, answered

What is the Moqui Framework used for?

Moqui is used to build custom enterprise applications: ERP systems, order management and fulfillment platforms, eCommerce backends, project and asset management tools, and multi-tenant SaaS products. It is most valuable where business processes are too specific for a packaged product but too complex to justify building enterprise data and service layers from scratch.

What Moqui solutions does HotWax Systems provide?

HotWax Systems delivers six core Moqui solution patterns: omnichannel order management and fulfillment, custom ERP and enterprise applications, ERP augmentation through microservices, integration and API layers, multi-tenant SaaS platform engineering, and AI and agentic workflow enablement. Engagements run from architecture and design through development, migration, cloud deployment and ongoing managed support.

Is Moqui free to use commercially?

Yes. Moqui Framework and Mantle Business Artifacts are released to the public domain under Creative Commons Zero (CC0-1.0) with a Grant of Patent License. There are no license fees, seat costs or usage restrictions, and you may embed Moqui inside a proprietary commercial product, fork it, or resell software built on it without attribution requirements.

How is Moqui different from Apache OFBiz?

Moqui is a complete rewrite rather than a fork, sharing the same architectural lineage of entity engine, service engine and screen framework, with a lighter runtime, a tighter API contract, a consolidated data model and built-in multi-tenancy. Apache OFBiz has the larger community and far more out-of-the-box application modules. Choose OFBiz when packaged modules fit your requirements; choose Moqui when you're building a custom system and want a clean architectural foundation. We build on both. See our Apache OFBiz development and consulting services.

Can Moqui integrate with our existing ERP and eCommerce systems?

Yes. Moqui was designed API-first, shipping over 400 REST endpoints along with GraphQL support and Apache Camel for message-based integration. We routinely connect Moqui to NetSuite, Shopify, warehouse management systems, payment and shipping providers, and enterprise data warehouses, either as a full backbone or as a focused service alongside systems you already run.

Is Moqui suitable for multi-tenant SaaS products?

It is one of Moqui's strongest use cases. Multi-instance provisioning with isolated databases is built into the framework, and Mantle includes subscription and access-management primitives. For independent software vendors, SaaS delivery becomes a configuration decision rather than a re-architecture project.

Do you provide Moqui support and maintenance for existing systems?

Yes. We take over existing Moqui deployments for support, maintenance, performance tuning, version upgrades and feature development, including systems originally built by other vendors or in-house teams. Engagements include dedicated account management rather than an anonymous ticket queue.
