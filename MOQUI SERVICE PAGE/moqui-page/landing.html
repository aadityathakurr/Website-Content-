The problem

You are not fighting your team. You are fighting the software.

Packaged ERP is built for the average business, and no retail operation is average. The gap between how the software works and how you actually sell gets absorbed by people, spreadsheets and overtime, until the gap is the operation.

  • The system cannot express how you fulfill. Ship-from-store, pre-orders, split shipments and returns live in spreadsheets beside the ERP because the ERP has no opinion about them.
  • Every integration is its own project. The storefront, the POS, the warehouse and the carrier each speak a different dialect, and the code holding them together is undocumented and owned by whoever wrote it.
  • Renewal is leverage against you. Seat counts and module fees climb every cycle, and the feature you need is always on next year's roadmap, behind a hundred other customers.
  • Peak season is a risk event, not a revenue event. The team spends the fourth quarter watching queues instead of selling, because nobody is sure what the system does at four times normal volume.
  • Inventory truth arrives too late to act on. By the time the numbers reconcile, the units are already oversold in one channel and sitting dead in another.
  • The fix quote comes back in years and millions. So the decision gets deferred another cycle, and the workarounds harden into policy.

This is not a you problem.

It is the normal end state of packaged ERP inside an omnichannel operation. The product was scoped before ship-from-store, before marketplaces, before same-day, and every year since has been absorbed by configuration and workarounds rather than architecture.

  • 50+ enterprise operations where we have walked into exactly this pattern
  • 20+ years watching packaged ERP hit the same wall in retail
  • 1 root cause every time: the business outgrew the vendor's data model

You do not need a bigger vendor. You need a data model that can hold your operation and an engineering team that can shape it.

Road one: buy a bigger package

  • Fastest to sign, slowest to fit
  • Your process bends to the product
  • License and seat costs compound yearly
  • Customization voids the upgrade path
  • You never own the code you depend on

Road two: build it from scratch

  • Perfect fit, eventually
  • The first year goes to plumbing, not product
  • Security, authorization and audit built twice
  • Budget and timeline are guesses
  • Key-person risk from the first commit

Road three: build on Moqui with HotWax

  • Start on a proven enterprise data model, not a blank file
  • Only your differentiators get built from scratch
  • Zero license fees, under a public domain license
  • Complete source code ownership from day one
  • A named architect who has done this before

How we solve it

Six things we build, and what each one buys you

Every engagement starts from the Moqui Framework and the Mantle business data model, so the entities, services and security your system needs already exist. We build the part that is actually yours.

01

Custom ERP on a proven data model

We build on Mantle Business Artifacts, an enterprise data model of roughly 390 entities covering parties, products, orders, inventory, shipments, invoices and accounting, with the service layer and authorization already in place.

Your operation's actual rules go on top of that as services and screens, not as a fork of somebody else's product.

What that means for you

You skip the year of data modeling that sinks from-scratch ERP projects, and you never negotiate with a vendor about whether your process is supported.

  • First production release in months
  • Your process, unmodified
  • No upgrade path to void
02

Omnichannel order management and fulfillment orchestration

One system that takes orders from every channel, decides where each one should be fulfilled from, and drives it through picking, packing and shipping. Ship from store, buy online and pick up in store, returns in store, pre-orders, split shipments and back orders are all first class.

What that means for you

The exceptions your team currently handles by hand become routing rules the system applies at three in the morning without anyone watching.

  • Store inventory becomes sellable inventory
  • Fewer split shipments, lower shipping cost
  • Peak season stops being a fire drill
03

An integration layer instead of a pile of connectors

Moqui exposes more than 400 REST endpoints out of the box, and every service we write is callable the same way. We put your storefront, POS, warehouse system, third party logistics providers, carriers and finance systems behind one documented contract.

That includes the integrations retail actually runs on: Shopify, NetSuite, point of sale, and shipping gateways.

What that means for you

Adding the next channel or the next warehouse is a scoped piece of work against a known interface, rather than an archaeology project.

  • One place to look when something breaks
  • New channels in weeks, not quarters
  • No undocumented glue owned by one person
04

Modernization off legacy ERP, without a big bang

We put new services alongside the existing system, move one process at a time, and keep both running until the old one has nothing left to do. Moqui's core is around 25MB and runs in a gigabyte of memory, so standing up a parallel environment is cheap enough to do it this way.

What that means for you

You get value from the first milestone instead of the last one, and there is never a weekend where the whole company is betting on a cutover.

  • Incremental cutover, reversible at each step
  • Budget released in stages, not up front
  • The legacy system retires quietly
05

Multi-tenant SaaS platform engineering

If what you are building is a product rather than an internal system, Moqui handles multi-tenancy, per-tenant data isolation and authorization at the framework level. We build the platform, the tenant onboarding and the administration around it.

What that means for you

You onboard your tenth customer on the same codebase as your first, which is the difference between a product and a set of installations.

  • One codebase, many tenants
  • Public domain license, so you can resell it
  • No per-seat cost passed through to you
06

AI-ready services and agentic workflows

Because every business operation is already a callable service with authorization attached, your operational data and actions can be exposed to language models and agents through an MCP server surface rather than through screen scraping.

What that means for you

The AI work you will be asked for next year lands on an architecture that was ready for it, instead of a two year integration project.

  • Operational data queryable by agents
  • Permissions enforced at the service layer
  • No parallel AI data platform to maintain
