How Apache OFBiz support works with HotWax.

Built by us, another vendor, or your own team, the path is the same. One team learns your system, fixes what is urgent first, and stays with you.

  1. 1

    Handover and access

    We start from your code repository and environments. If another vendor holds them today, we help you request a complete handover.

  2. 2

    Review

    We study your release, custom code, integrations, and infrastructure, and write down what we find, so the knowledge stops living in one person's head.

  3. 3

    Stabilize

    We fix what hurts most first: blocking bugs, pending security releases, and the slow spots your users feel every day. Where it helps, we can overlap with your current vendor before taking over.

  4. 4

    Ongoing support

    The same engineers stay on your system for upgrades, fixes, and whatever the business needs next. Clients can keep 3 to 4 HotWax engineers as their permanent team.
